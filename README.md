# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

A deterministic program that writes a 99-word monologue for a fantasy villain
who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses
and the small deflections — the brazier smoke, the breakfast-time heroes, the
endless ink — never in a plain statement that he is weary. He still has a
scheme and a history: three hundred years holding the mountain, kingdoms
signed away before lunch, and paperwork that outlived them all. The sting
lands in the last line, over a cup of tea.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT`).
Two runs produce byte-identical output: 540 characters, 99 words, inside the
gate of 1–600 characters.
